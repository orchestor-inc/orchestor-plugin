## Separate observed change from causal attribution

Avoids recommendations stated as proven effects.

**Incorrect:** Claim a page edit caused higher citation rate because the next report increased.

**Correct:** Report the observed before/after difference and plausible confounders. Describe the intervention and follow-up measurement as a hypothesis test.

Platform/model changes, prompt mix, source refreshes, and sampling variation can change results. A repeated cohort improves comparison but does not by itself establish causality.
