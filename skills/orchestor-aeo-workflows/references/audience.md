# Adapt presentation, not evidence

## Agency

Use a separate workspace/evidence set for each client. A project name is not a
tenant boundary. Return a client-specific summary with dates, scope, evidence,
and decisions. Do not combine customer data for a benchmark without authorization.

## In-house marketing team

Connect findings to the team's brand, prompts, and current priorities. Preserve
ownership and status from existing records. Do not invent assignees, deadlines,
or strategy decisions to make a report look complete.

## Content strategist

Tie suggested edits to the actual page, query intent, and answer/source evidence.
Provide a reviewable content proposal rather than silently publishing it. Keep
editorial quality and factual support explicit.

These are presentations of the same product workflows, not separate privileged
skills. No private company corpus or internal operating procedure is required.
