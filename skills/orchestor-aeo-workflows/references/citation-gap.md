# Citation-gap investigation

Resolve the specific prompt/topic, target brand, competitors, and period. Read
visibility and citation/source data under the same scope. Inspect the actual
answers and cited pages before choosing a remedy.

Classify the observed evidence: insufficient measurement; entity/brand ambiguity;
first-party content mismatch; a recurring third-party source; or another supported
explanation. These are investigation categories, not automatic causal diagnoses.

Use public research for the relevant source pages when necessary. Keep the source's
statements separate from independent verification. Recommend one bounded change
with an acceptance condition and a comparable follow-up plan.

**Incorrect:** prescribe a fixed 4–8 week result or guarantee a citation increase.

**Correct:** state the observation cadence and uncertainty. If the user asks to
track the work, create an Orchestor Issue with source evidence. Do not require an
internal /lp-info skill, publish content, or contact third parties as an implicit
part of the investigation.
