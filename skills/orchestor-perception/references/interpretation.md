# Attribute, sentiment, and truth are distinct

“Affordable” appearing beside a brand is an attributed characteristic in the
observed answer. It is not necessarily a positive review, a verified pricing fact,
a unique product advantage, or proof of what caused a citation.

**Incorrect:** call a frequently attributed feature a proven competitive advantage
without inspecting the actual quote or competing evidence.

**Correct:** report occurrence/answer counts and the quotes, retain the reporting
scope, and distinguish attributed topics from sentiment. Rankings may fix their
own grouping/order; do not assume sorting or arbitrary metric fields will change
that contract.

Absence from a brand-mention filter is not inferred negative sentiment. Missing
observations and small samples must remain explicit limitations.
