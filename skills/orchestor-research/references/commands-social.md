# CLI command reference

Catalog snapshot: 2026-10-03, CLI source version 0.6.2. These names were
resolved against the public CLI registry. This proves routing coverage, not
authenticated execution or availability in every published binary. Read the
installed leaf command’s `--help` for arguments, schema, permissions and limits.

| Command | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `orc research linkedin companies get` | Get LinkedIn company |
| `orc research linkedin companies people list` | List LinkedIn company people |
| `orc research linkedin companies posts list` | List LinkedIn company posts |
| `orc research linkedin people search` | Search LinkedIn people |
| `orc research linkedin posts comments list` | List LinkedIn post comments |
| `orc research linkedin posts get` | Get LinkedIn post |
| `orc research linkedin posts search` | Search LinkedIn posts |
| `orc research linkedin profiles get` | Get LinkedIn profile |
| `orc research linkedin profiles posts list` | List LinkedIn profile posts |
| `orc research meta-ads ads get` | Get Meta ad |
| `orc research meta-ads ads search` | Search Meta ads |
| `orc research meta-ads advertisers ads list` | List Meta advertiser ads |
| `orc research meta-ads advertisers search` | Search Meta advertisers |
| `orc research reddit comments search` | Search Reddit comments |
| `orc research reddit posts comments list` | List Reddit post comments |
| `orc research reddit posts get` | Get Reddit post |
| `orc research reddit posts search` | Search Reddit posts |
| `orc research reddit subreddits get` | Get Reddit community |
| `orc research reddit subreddits posts list` | List Reddit community posts |
| `orc research reddit subreddits posts search` | Search within a Reddit community |
| `orc research tiktok comments replies list` | List TikTok comment replies |
| `orc research tiktok profiles get` | Get TikTok profile |
| `orc research tiktok profiles videos list` | List TikTok profile videos |
| `orc research tiktok users search` | Search TikTok users |
| `orc research tiktok videos comments list` | List TikTok video comments |
| `orc research tiktok videos get` | Get TikTok video |
| `orc research tiktok videos search` | Search TikTok videos |
| `orc research tiktok videos transcript get` | Get TikTok captions |
| `orc research x accounts get` | Get X account |
| `orc research x accounts post-observations import` | Import previously acquired X observations |
| `orc research x accounts posts list` | List X account posts |
| `orc research x posts analytics` | Analyze saved X posts |
| `orc research x posts get` | Get X post |
| `orc research x posts list` | Rank and search saved X posts |
| `orc research youtube channels get` | Get YouTube channel |
| `orc research youtube channels search` | Search YouTube channels |
| `orc research youtube channels videos list` | List YouTube channel videos |
| `orc research youtube transcripts get` | Get YouTube transcripts in a batch |
| `orc research youtube videos get` | Get YouTube video |
| `orc research youtube videos search` | Search YouTube videos |
| `orc research youtube videos transcript get` | Get YouTube transcript |
