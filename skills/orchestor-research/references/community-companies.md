# Communities, press releases, and startup research

Choose the source family that matches the evidence:

- Qiita and Zenn for developer articles, topics, users, and community activity.
- note for articles, creators, tags, interests, and trends.
- PR TIMES for company announcements, releases, categories, and publication dates.
- YC and a16z speedrun for their listed companies, founders, launches, or posts.

Source-specific lists and searches have different filtering and pagination.
Resolve the actual source ID before fetching details. Do not assume a company
appears in a program because a founder name matches, or treat a press release as
independent reporting.

**Incorrect:** combine release dates and acquisition dates as one timeline, or
call a partial list the complete market.

**Correct:** retain event/publication timestamps separately from capture time,
state which sources and pages were searched, and report the stopping bound.
A launch post, directory entry, and legal company record support different claims.
