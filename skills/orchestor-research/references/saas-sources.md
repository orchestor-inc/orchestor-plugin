# Japanese SaaS research sources

| Source | Useful starting point |
| --- | --- |
| BOXIL | categories, products, rankings, product detail, reviews, search |
| ITreview | product detail, reviews, alternatives, comparisons, categories, market graph |
| ITトレンド | category directory, products, rankings, issues, market |
| ASPIC | categories, services, articles, authors, search |

Use the source's own product/vendor/category identifier after discovery. Do not
pass one service's ID to another provider or assume same-named products are the
same edition, country offering, or vendor.

**Incorrect:** call a vendor's marketing statement an independently verified
feature, or compare aggregate ratings without recording source and sample size.

**Correct:** separate vendor statements, reviewer reports, and your synthesis.
Record source URLs/IDs, acquisition date, and version/plan when available. If a
review endpoint returns no evidence, do not invent a review or zero rating.

For alternatives or rankings, record how the source defines the category and
ordering. A source ranking is not an objective market-wide quality ranking.
Load only the product detail/review slices needed for the requested comparison.
